Calibrate this camera

Optional. Without calibration the app assumes a 62° horizontal field of view, which is close enough for a model to sit convincingly on a marker. Calibrating measures your camera instead, which mainly improves how well the model stays put near the edges of the frame.

The result is stored in this browser only (localStorage) and no image is uploaded or kept.

1. Print the board

Generated by OpenCV itself, so it matches the detector exactly. Print at 100% scale, then measure one black square with a ruler and enter the real measurement below — the measurement, not the intended size.

2. Capture views

Take at least 8 shots of the flat board from clearly different angles and distances, filling a good part of the frame, including some where the board is near a corner. Shots that are all from the same angle give a confident-looking but wrong result.
